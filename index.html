Free Clip Desk is a personal Windows application for acquiring permitted campaign footage, creating short captioned videos on the computer, and publishing through accounts the owner explicitly connects.

Local processing

Speech recognition and video rendering use locally installed Whisper and FFmpeg components. The runtime does not need GPT Plus or a paid inference subscription.

Authorized publishing

The optional YouTube connection checks the selected channel and uploads prepared clips with their title, caption, attribution and campaign disclosure.

Why Google access is requested

YouTube upload permission lets the application publish the prepared video. Read-only YouTube access verifies the selected channel and the app’s uploads, and checks configured source videos for creator identity, publication date and reuse license. The connection is controlled by the owner and can be revoked in the Google Account settings.

This deployment is for the owner of the All in 1 channel. This website describes the application; it does not host account sign-in, uploaded media or an editing service.

Local acquisition and clipping are available. Background publishing requires channel authorization. Regular creator clips and paid campaign clips have been published in live testing. Bounty-link submission uses the paired Chrome helper; paid uploads are held when the submission connection or its outcome is uncertain.

What “free” means

The local application has no software subscription or per-clip charge. It uses the owner’s computer, storage, electricity and internet connection. Social-platform quotas apply. Campaign services may deduct fees from earnings and decide whether submissions qualify for payment.
