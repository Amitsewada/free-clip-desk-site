Privacy Policy

This policy describes Free Clip Desk, the personal Windows video workflow operated by Amit Kumar. Contact amitkumarsewada@gmail.com about the application or this policy.

Google access and purpose

Connecting YouTube uses Google’s authorization screen. The application requests youtube.upload to upload videos selected by the configured workflow and youtube.readonly to check the selected channel’s name and identifier and support checks on the app’s uploads. The app uses these permissions for the connected publishing workflow. It does not request Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or YouTube Analytics access.

The app handles OAuth client configuration, access and refresh tokens, the connected channel’s name and identifier, video titles, descriptions, visibility settings, uploaded video identifiers and URLs. Your Google password is entered only in Google’s own interface and is not requested by the local application.

Footage and local files

The workflow handles configured source URLs, downloaded or supplied footage, local speech transcripts, subtitles, exported clips, campaign rules, queue records, posted links, submission deadlines and operational logs. Speech recognition and rendering run locally. They do not send transcripts to a hosted language-model or transcription service.

Media and job files remain in the configured workspace until the owner removes them. Storage limits pause further acquisition; they do not automatically delete existing files. If the workspace is inside OneDrive or another synchronized folder, that service may synchronize the files according to the owner’s settings.

Credential storage

Google client configuration and account tokens are encrypted using Windows DPAPI under %LOCALAPPDATA%\FreeClipDesk\secrets, outside the media workspace. Protection depends on the security of the Windows account and computer. The application can refresh an authorized token to keep its configured publishing connection working.

Sharing and service providers

Authorized uploads send the clip and its publishing metadata to YouTube. Optional Instagram publishing, when configured, sends those items to Meta. Source monitoring and acquisition contact the configured source services. The initial installation may download open model files and software packages from their distributors.

A bounty submission connection, when installed and enabled, uses the owner’s existing signed-in campaign-service session to check the configured brief and submit posted links. Its local browser pairing and queue messages contain campaign details, posted URLs and submission status. A loopback broker is designed to listen only on the computer itself. This connection is under implementation and is held inactive until configured and verified.

Content Rewards’ separate social-account connection is managed by Content Rewards. Its access to account statistics and audience data is governed by its own authorization and privacy policy. Free Clip Desk does not receive that service’s Google tokens.

Limited Use and AI

Free Clip Desk follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising profiling, or used to train or improve AI or machine-learning models. Local transcription runs an existing model for the requested video-processing task.

Controls, retention and deletion

The owner can stop automation, revoke the application’s access in Google Account permissions, remove local encrypted connection files, and delete media and job records from the workspace. Removing local files does not delete already published posts or records held by social platforms, campaign services or a configured backup provider. Those services provide their own deletion controls.

There is no application-operated remote account database or data-sale service. Local records are retained until the owner deletes them or changes the application. Changes to these practices will require updating this policy and the application’s corresponding notices.

This information website

The static information pages are prepared for GitHub Pages hosting. They contain no application-provided analytics, advertising scripts, account forms or tracking cookies. GitHub may process website requests and security logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
