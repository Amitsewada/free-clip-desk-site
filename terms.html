Terms of Use

Free Clip Desk is a personal Windows application operated by Amit Kumar for a configured video-clipping and publishing workflow. Contact amitkumarsewada@gmail.com about the application.

Authorized content and accounts

Use footage you own or are authorized to process and post. Campaign permission, source restrictions, advertising disclosures, audience requirements and each service’s rules still apply. Connecting an account authorizes the app only for the permissions shown by that service. The app checks the configured YouTube channel before publishing.

Automation and review

The workflow can acquire configured sources, transcribe speech, select passages, render captions and queue clips. Publishing depends on valid authorization and configured campaign rules. Bounty posting is held unless a submission path is available. Transcription and automatic selection can contain errors. A generated clip, successful upload or submitted link is not an approval for payment.

Costs and resources

The local application charges no software subscription or per-clip fee. The owner supplies the computer, storage, electricity and internet. Third-party services apply their own quotas, campaign deductions, eligibility and payout terms. This application does not collect or transfer campaign payouts.

Operation

The application runs while the owner’s computer and required connections are available. Expired permissions, source changes, exhausted campaign budgets, platform changes or uncertain submission results can stop the workflow. The owner can stop the worker and revoke account access. The application is provided as available, without a promise of uninterrupted operation, views, approvals or earnings.

The Privacy Policy explains how account information, credentials, media and job records are handled. These information pages are not a public media-upload or editing service.
